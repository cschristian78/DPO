EXCL, DIFF, and Exposure

EP_1 through EP_15 are the return-period columns. EXCL has the return-period losses, AAL, and standard deviation of the portfolio with that risk removed. DIFF is the portfolio minus EXCL for each of those metrics. Exposure lists the location attributes gathered from the EDM. Compare joins EXCL and DIFF for the risk ids you select. Click a column heading to sort. Search matches the {{ dashboard.loss_level }} id. Link filters carries every risk id selected on Compare to EXCL and DIFF. Exposure stays the full portfolio. Unlink shows every row.
